We have spent over a decade building communications infrastructure that businesses depend on every minute of every day—with a singular obsession for uptime, security, and the human on the other end of the line.

Founded in 2012, Switchboard Plus began with a simple belief: small and mid-sized businesses should not have to choose between enterprise-grade reliability and a bill they can actually understand.

Today, we support healthcare practices, law firms, contact centers, and growing teams that would rather not babysit a phone system. We pick up our own phones. We answer our own tickets. And we put a thousand dollars on the line every time we sign you up.
